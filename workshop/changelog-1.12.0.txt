1.12.0 — the Witching Pact

New
- Cursed Deck (without Overcursed): Neow offers the Witching Pact. Pick 1 of 3 boss relics; the price is the Witching Hour, which replaces your Ascender's Bane (or joins your deck below Ascension 5). Skip the relics to refuse.
- The Forge + Cursed Deck: the Witching Hour now fuses and can be duplicated, up to +9, while staying Eternal. From +1, held in hand at the end of an odd turn it costs you 15 HP, 9 more per level (87 at +9). From +5, your other cards cost 2 more instead of 1, and its Unholy Grimoire stun lasts 2 turns. It never gets the Odd, Even or Echo defect.
