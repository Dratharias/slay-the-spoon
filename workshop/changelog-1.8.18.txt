1.8.18 — Folie à deux chest view, Matriochka final card, Watcher, Spoon and Forge fixes (1.8.11 to 1.8.18)

- Folie à deux: the chest view now fits the screen (the Continue button could end below it), and every card in it shows its real title and text instead of a "Broken Card" placeholder.
- Matriochka no longer forces a card pick in the victory room after the run's last boss (the Architect). Card picks after earlier bosses, and after the act 3 boss when an act 4 or the Spoon act follows, are unchanged.
- Fixed a crash when the Watcher enters a rest site during a long co-op run.
- Fixed Watcher rest site animation errors: her own idle animation plays without the donor character's missing act animations.
- Fixed Brilliance trying to load an attack effect that does not exist in Slay the Spire 2.
- Fixed the Spoon's Digest rest site option trying to load a missing icon; it shows the same heal icon as Feast.
- King Midas now prevents Forge's Duplicate service from spending enough gold to ruin the buyer, like merchant and rest site purchases.
- These fixes came from co-op run logs, owner reports and backlog review. The bot code is not included in the Workshop build.
