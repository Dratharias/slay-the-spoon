1.3.7 — Spoon for the whole team

- No more supporters: every player fights the Spoon. Spoon-Feed is gone.
- The Spoon act opens on the team's total max HP: 300 solo, 550 for two, 750 for three, 900 for four.
- New Workshop preview and description.
- Appetite everywhere: every monster steals 1 Appetite per hit on a player, and the Spoonful removes Appetite from any monster.
- Slay the Spoon: a mode card on the solo and multiplayer host screens starts a Standard run in Spoon mode; the Custom Run option is renamed Slay the Spoon.
- The Spire Souvenir now works for the Necrobinder and the Regent too (colorless cards from both games).
- Shorter Custom Run descriptions.
