[b]Alpha, friends only.[/b] Not tested much in game yet: expect bugs, and keep your saves backed up.

Slay the Spoon brings Slay the Spire 1 cards into Slay the Spire 2 and adds run options that the lobby host sets in the [b]Custom Run[/b] screen. Every player in a co-op lobby needs the same version of the mod.

[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3737335127]BaseLib[/url][/b] (3.4.7 or later). Steam offers to subscribe to it with this item.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Monster HP[/b] and [b]Monster Damage[/b] options in Custom Run, 25% to 500%, synced in co-op and kept in saves.
[*][b]Slay the Spire 1 Cards[/b]: rewards, shops and card generation offer Slay the Spire 1 cards instead of Slay the Spire 2 ones.
[*][b]Spire Souvenir[/b]: a relic that lets its owner find cards from both games. A Custom Run option gives it at the start.
[*]Slay the Spire 1 cards for the Ironclad, the Silent and the Defect, and colorless cards for every character, with their original art and numbers.
[*][b]Neow's Blessings[/b]: brings back Neow's usual starting choice in Custom Runs.
[*][b]The Watcher[/b] as a sixth character (Custom Run, with a Slay the Spire 1 card option ticked).
[*]Slay the Spire 1 events (Vampires, Augmenter, The Nest) can appear in act 2.
[*][b]Act 4, The Ending[/b]: collect the Ruby, Emerald and Sapphire keys to face the Corrupt Heart. Unlocked by reaching act 3 at Ascension 10, or with a Custom Run option.
[*][b]Merchant Loans[/b] (co-op Custom Run): ask your allies for gold at the merchant, with or without interest.
[*][b]Character Color[/b] (pause menu): tint your character and your map drawings; co-op partners see it.
[*]Optional one-way copy of your regular progress into the modded profile. Your regular save is only read, never written.
[/list]

Standard and Daily runs stay vanilla, except that the Spire Souvenir can drop in Standard runs and Act 4 opens there once unlocked.
