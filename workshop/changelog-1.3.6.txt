1.3.6 — Spoon balance and fixes

- Rebalanced the Spoon: 1200 starting HP, 25/35 base attack damage, Banquet healing reduced to 30.
- Banquet deals 8 fixed damage through Block; Skimmer prevents this and max HP loss, lasting until Banquet.
- Melon Baller now costs 1 energy. Full max HP growth and 1 Strength per 5 max HP from the combat baseline remain.
- Attacks steal 1 Appetite per player hit, even when fully blocked.
- Fixed promoted relic prices, short-act maps (Spoon and Heart), and borrowed combat power icons.
- Removed the test launcher; existing test saves remain compatible.
