# The Witching Pact (Cursed Deck Neow option)

Owner calls, 2026-11-24.

## Rule

- Cursed Deck runs **without Overcursed** get one more Neow page: the Witching Pact. Overcursed already hands out the
  Witching Hour, so it gets no pact.
- The page holds one option (vanilla modifier pages hold one). It opens a chest of **3 StS1 boss relics** (the boss chest
  pool of the "StS1 Boss Relics" option, for this player's character, act 1 rules) on the vanilla "Choose a Relic" screen.
  - **Taking a relic seals the pact:** the Witching Hour replaces the player's Ascender's Bane. Below Ascension 5 (no
    bane), the Witching Hour joins the deck instead.
  - **Skipping refuses the pact:** no relic, no curse.
- Each player has their own Neow and their own pact; the chest rolls on a per-player stream named after the pact, from
  the run seed, so vanilla seeds stay untouched.
- With other Neow modifier options (Draft, Starting Chest…) the pages follow the modifier order; with Neow's Blessings
  the blessings come after them, as before.

## Text

Short, like the other Neow options: the title names the pact, the description says what taking a relic costs.
