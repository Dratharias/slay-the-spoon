# Step 6 — Import the StS1 Ending

## Scope and current status

Owner request: import StS1 Act 4 with Ruby, Emerald and Sapphire keys, a permanent Standard
run unlock and a Custom Run activation option. Branch: `step-6-act4`, based on the completed merchant-loan implementation.

The first import stages 23 original assets and selected EN/FR text for three monsters and five
powers in `data/sts1/act4/`. It does not yet add a playable act or expose an unfinished lobby option.
No Java class files or decompiled source are stored in the repository.

## Approved unlock rule (owner clarification, 2026-10-29)

- The first time the player **reaches Act 3 in a Standard run at Ascension 10**, Act 4 becomes
  permanently available. Reaching Act 3 is the trigger; beating its boss or winning the run is not required.
- A subsequent defeat does not revoke the unlock.
- Once unlocked, Act 4 is available at **every ascension level**, including Ascension 0.
  Ascension 10 is only the initial unlock requirement; it is not a recurring access requirement.
- A Custom Run option can activate Act 4 without this prior unlock.
- This is availability of **Act 4 across ascension levels**, not availability in every act.
- The permanent unlock is distinct from collecting the three keys within a run.
- Daily runs remain unchanged. Store mod progression without writing to the regular save.
- The unlock and preview-gated run flag are implemented in WP3. Public access remains disabled until WP6.

## Constraints

- Standard runs use the permanent unlock above; Custom Runs use their activation option. Daily runs remain unchanged.
- No regular-save writes. Progression and keys must survive modded save/reload and co-op resume.
- No install, push or Workshop upload as part of this import.
- EN/FR for all new UI. Extracted StS1 formatting is source data, not ready-to-use StS2 localization.
- Existing monster HP/damage modifiers must apply exactly once to the imported enemies.
- Co-op key ownership (owner decision, 2026-10-29): one team-wide set, as in StS1. A key collected by any
  player counts for the whole team.
- Enemy mechanics must be verified from local StS1 code before porting, including ascension changes.
  Do not guess the Heart's attack order, Invincible cap or scaling rules.

## Local source reconnaissance

- Installed StS1 archive contains the Heart under `images/npcs/heart/`, and Shield/Spear under
  `images/monsters/theEnding/{shield,spear}/`.
- All three animation skeletons declare Spine 3.4.02. Preserve atlas, texture and skeleton together;
  inspect the StS2 animation loader and conversion requirements before importing them into Godot.
- Source keys: top-panel red/green/blue sprites and slots; large Ruby/Emerald/Sapphire key images.
- Ending background atlas plus its three JPEG pages are included.
- The ending stinger is included; this is not a claim that all combat music has been imported.
- StS2 `ActModel.CreateMap` directly creates a `StandardActMap`: the short Ending map needs
  explicit integration rather than just overriding a virtual map factory.
- StS2 `RunManager.EnterNextAct` compares the current index with `State.Acts.Count - 1`,
  then enters TheArchitect. `WinRun` casts the room to an Architect event and triggers its victory.
  Preserve this end-of-run contract or replace it deliberately; do not bypass it with an arbitrary win call.
- RunManager assigns DoubleBoss to the last act in its list. Appending Act 4 must preserve
  the intended act-3 double boss and must not accidentally give the Heart a second boss.
- StS2 saves serialize the act list and generated maps. The optional final act and key state must
  be restored consistently on every peer before progression checks.
