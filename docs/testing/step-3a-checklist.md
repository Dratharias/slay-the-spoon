# Step 3a — in-game checklist

Tick when checked in game. "Base / +" means the card before and after upgrade.
Quickest way: Custom Run, Silent, "Spire Souvenir" (Hybrid: the Silent starts with the relic) or "Slay the Spire 1 Cards" ticked.
Fastest: in combat, open the dev console (` key) and type `sts1card STS1_BANE` (Tab completes the id; add `Deck` to put it in the deck). Vanilla helpers: `energy 9`, `godmode`, `upgrade 0` (leftmost card in hand), `power POISON_POWER 5 1` (5 Poison on creature 1; the index runs over every creature in the fight, so try 1 or 2 for the first enemy).

## Ironclad cards whose StS2 version sits outside the Ironclad pool

StS2's Clash, Dual Wield and Entrench are Event cards and Shockwave is colorless, so the Ironclad's StS1-only and Hybrid pools carry the StS1 copies.

- [ ] StS1-only and Hybrid, Ironclad: Clash, Dual Wield, Entrench and Shockwave can show up in card rewards (StS1 art).
- [ ] Clash (0): playable only when every card in hand is an Attack (glows gold then); 14 / 18 damage.
- [ ] Dual Wield (1): choose an Attack or Power in hand; 1 / 2 copies of it are added to the hand. The prompt reads "Choose a card to Copy." / « Choisissez une carte à Copier. ».
- [ ] Entrench (2 / 1): doubles your Block.
- [ ] Shockwave (2, Exhaust): 3 / 5 Weak and Vulnerable to ALL enemies.

## Silent attacks

- [ ] Bane (1): 7 / 10 damage; hits a second time if the enemy had Poison (glows gold when an enemy has Poison).
- [ ] Choke (2): 12 damage; each card you play this turn makes that enemy lose 3 / 5 HP.
- [ ] Die Die Die (1, Exhaust): 13 / 17 damage to ALL enemies.
- [ ] All-Out Attack (1): 10 / 14 damage to ALL enemies, then discards 1 random card from your hand.
- [ ] Quick Slash (1): 8 / 12 damage, draw 1.
- [ ] Riddle with Holes (2): 3 / 4 damage 5 times.
- [ ] Unload (1): 14 / 18 damage, then discards every non-Attack card in hand.
- [ ] Eviscerate (3): costs 1 less for each card you discarded this turn (Survivor, Acrobatics, All-Out Attack…), down to 0; the cost shown on the card drops as you discard; back to 3 next turn. 7 / 9 damage 3 times.
- [ ] Masterful Stab (0): costs 1 more each time you lost HP this combat (the cost shown goes up after an unblocked hit); 12 / 16 damage. Back to 0 next combat.
- [ ] Flying Knee (1): 8 / 11 damage; next turn you start with 1 extra energy.
- [ ] Glass Knife (1): 8 / 12 damage twice; each play lowers its damage by 2 for the rest of the combat (never below 0); back to full next combat.
- [ ] Heel Hook (1): 5 / 8 damage; if the enemy was Weak, gain 1 energy and draw 1 (glows gold when an enemy is Weak).
- [ ] Sneaky Strike (2): 12 / 16 damage; if you discarded a card this turn, gain 2 energy (glows gold then).
- [ ] Endless Agony (0, Exhaust): 4 / 6 damage; drawing it adds a copy to your hand (the copy doesn't copy itself again).
- [ ] StS1-only and Hybrid, Silent: Flechettes, Grand Finale, Skewer and Sucker Punch show up only in their StS1 version (StS1 art).
- [ ] Flechettes (1): 4 / 6 damage per Skill in hand; in combat the text shows the hit count.
- [ ] Grand Finale (0): playable only with an empty draw pile (glows gold then); 50 / 60 damage to ALL enemies, with the StS2 animation.
- [ ] Skewer (X): 7 / 10 damage X times.
- [ ] Sucker Punch (1): 7 / 9 damage, 1 / 2 Weak.

## Silent skills

- [ ] Catalyst (1, Exhaust): doubles / triples the enemy's Poison (nothing happens without Poison).
- [ ] Concentrate (0): choose 3 / 2 cards to discard, then gain 2 energy.
- [ ] Corpse Explosion (2): 6 / 9 Poison and a "Corpse Explosion" debuff (bomb icon); when that enemy dies, every other enemy takes damage equal to its Max HP (Strength doesn't add to it). Two copies on one enemy: twice its Max HP.
- [ ] Crippling Cloud (2, Exhaust): 4 / 7 Poison and 2 Weak to ALL enemies.
- [ ] Doppelganger (X, Exhaust): next turn, draw X (X+1) more cards and gain X (X+1) energy. At X = 0 the base version does nothing.
- [ ] Phantasmal Killer (1 / 0): next turn, your Attacks deal double damage (vanilla Shadow Step's buff, then Double Damage).
- [ ] Setup (1 / 0): choose a card in hand; it goes on top of the draw pile and costs 0 when you draw it, until you play it. The prompt reads "Choose a card to put on top of your Draw Pile." / « Choisissez une carte à placer sur le dessus de votre Pioche. ».
- [ ] Terror (1 / 0, Exhaust): 99 Vulnerable.
- [ ] StS1-only and Hybrid, Silent: Alchemize, Distraction and Outmaneuver show up in their StS1 version (StS2's are outside the Silent pool).
- [ ] Alchemize (1 / 0, Exhaust): gives a random potion.
- [ ] Distraction (1 / 0, Exhaust): adds a random Silent Skill to your hand, free this turn (in StS1-only, a StS1 Skill).
- [ ] Outmaneuver (1): next turn, gain 2 / 3 energy.
- [ ] Blade Dance (1, no Exhaust): adds 3 / 4 Shivs to your hand.
- [ ] Calculated Gamble (0, Exhaust; upgraded: no Exhaust): discards your hand, then draws that many cards.
- [ ] Reflex (Unplayable): when a card discards it (Survivor, Acrobatics, Calculated Gamble…), draw 2 / 3. Discarding it at end of turn does nothing.
- [ ] Tactician (Unplayable): when a card discards it, gain 1 / 2 energy. Discarding it at end of turn does nothing.

## Silent powers

- [ ] A Thousand Cuts (2): whenever you play a card, 1 / 2 damage to ALL enemies (Phantom Blades icon). The copy that applies it doesn't trigger it; a second copy deals the old amount, then stacks.
- [ ] Wraith Form (3): 2 / 3 Intangible; at the END of your turn, lose 1 Dexterity (Wraith Form icon, red debuff).
- [ ] StS1-only and Hybrid, Silent: Caltrops and Envenom show up in their StS1 version.
- [ ] Caltrops (1): 3 / 5 Thorns.
- [ ] Envenom (2 / 1): whenever an Attack deals unblocked damage, apply 1 Poison.
- [ ] French titles of the namesakes match StS2's: Astucieux (Outmaneuver), Réflexes, Embrocher (Skewer), Tactique (Tactician), Envenimer.
