# Forged curses (1.10.0) — in-game checklist

Custom Run with **The Forge** and **Cursed Deck** ticked unless a line says otherwise. Nothing here is tested in game yet.

## Fusion and levels

- [ ] Fuse two Bad Luck to +1 then +2 at a rest site: the card reads 27, and played with the Grimoire the enemy loses 27 HP.
- [ ] The merchant's Duplicate button offers curses (not Eternal ones) and makes a +0 copy.
- [ ] Ascender's Bane, Greed and other Eternal curses are never offered by Fuse or Duplicate.
- [ ] A curse reaching +3 shows one purple defect line; +6 shows two and gains Retain; +9 shows three and gains Innate.
- [ ] Smith, Armaments or Black Censer on a +3 curse does nothing (ordinary upgrades stop at +1).

## Defects

- [ ] Odd / Even / Late / Demanding: the curse refuses to play while the condition holds, with the defect thought bubble.
- [ ] Heavy: the Grimoire cost shows 2 more.
- [ ] Bloody: playing it at low HP costs HP equal to its level (can kill).
- [ ] Echo on Debt: the owner loses half the gold the play gave.
- [ ] Contagious: a random curse lands in the draw pile.
- [ ] Ravenous: a random card leaves the hand for the exhaust pile.
- [ ] Spiteful Regret (or Bad Luck) left in hand hits twice at end of turn.

## Saves and co-op

- [ ] Save and reload with a +7 curse that has defects: level and defect lines unchanged.
- [ ] Co-op: the client sees the same level and defects on the host's forged curse, and plays stay in sync.
- [ ] Co-op, long run: a Spiteful curse left in hand at end of turn hits twice on both clients (no desync).

## Modes alone

- [ ] The Forge alone: curses never fuse, no Duplicate on curses.
- [ ] Cursed Deck alone: curses stop at +1, no defects.
